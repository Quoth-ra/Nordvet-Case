# Findings for Mette

What the VetOne data shows about the clinics' no-shows. Data as of 14 November 2026. "Last 28 days" means 17 October to 13 November; the history goes back to 6 August. The numbers change a little with every run, and the Power BI report always shows the current ones.

## In short

1. **About 1 in 10 expected clients doesn't come.** In the last 28 days there were 1,701 no-shows out of 17,063 expected visits (10.0%), plus 439 late cancellations. Together that is 834 lost vet hours, about 35 hours on every working day.
2. **Four clinics stand out:** Aarhus C (26.6%), Odense C (23.6%), Hillerød (23.0%) and København V (16.7%). The other 34 clinics are between 6.0% and 12.9%.
3. **Odense C is new to this group.** It was around 9% in August and September. Since October it has been between 15% and 33% a week, and above 22% in four of the last five weeks. Something changed there, and it is worth asking what. The other three have been high since August.
4. **It is the clinics, not what they offer.** The four have the same mix of services as everyone else, but more no-shows in every service.
5. **Routine visits are missed most:** follow-ups 14.1% and vaccinations 12.1%, against 4.7% for surgery. Reminders would help most here.
6. **The highest rate is not the biggest loss.** København V loses the most vet hours of all clinics, because it is large. And the four clinics together account for only 18% of the lost hours: most lost time is spread across the whole network.

The four clinics' mix is almost identical to the rest (about 44% consultations, 20% vaccinations, 15% follow-ups), so the mix doesn't explain their rates.

Surgery is rarely missed, but each missed surgery costs 90 minutes. It is 5% of the lost appointments but 21% of the lost hours.

## What does not explain it

- **Day of the week:** Mondays are a little higher (11.5%) than other days (8.7-9.9%), but no day is a problem on its own.
- **Country:** Denmark 10.2% and Sweden 9.4%. All four high clinics are Danish.
- **Particular patients:** since August, 724 patients missed two or more visits. About as many would by chance, given how often their clinic's clients miss (734). There is no group of repeat offenders to target. Patients also never move between clinics.
- **Bad days:** the network has stayed between 9% and 11% every week since August, and the four clinics are high on most days, not on a few.

## Late cancellations

2.4% of all booked slots are cancelled less than 24 hours ahead, and 3.8% earlier. One clinic stands out: **Malmö Centrum has no late cancellation at all** in 28 days, while the others have 1-5%. That probably means cancellations are recorded differently there, not that nobody cancels late.

## How to read these numbers

- **Yesterday's no-shows are mostly an estimate.** Staff record no-shows the next working day, so the report counts an appointment still open 5 hours after its start as a no-show. On 13 November, 57 of the 75 no-shows were not yet confirmed in VetOne. About 4% of these turn out to be visits recorded late, and they correct themselves within days.
- **17% of no-shows are recorded in VetOne as cancellations made after the appointment had started.** The report counts them as no-shows.
- **Lost vet hours are not money.** They are the booked minutes of no-shows and late cancellations. With a price per service, the report could show lost revenue.
- **A single clinic-day is too small to judge.** A clinic has around 18 expected visits a day, so one more no-show moves its daily rate by 5 points. Rank clinics on 28 days, and use the daily view to see whether a problem is lasting.
- **The 24-hour limit for a late cancellation is an assumption** and can be changed.

## Questions for Nordvet

1. **What changed at Odense C in October?** For example staff, opening hours, or the reminder routine.
2. **How do the four clinics remind clients**, compared with clinics around 6%?
3. **Do Aarhus C, Hillerød and København V record no-shows the same way as the others?** A different routine could make a clinic look worse than it is.
4. **How does Malmö Centrum record late cancellations?**
5. **Is 24 hours the right limit for a late cancellation?**
6. **Is there a price per service,** so lost hours can become lost revenue?
